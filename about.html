Built on trust

Your partner in every layer.

PK Holding is a trusted partner for high-quality building and interior solutions, serving residential and commercial projects with a carefully selected range of materials.

We specialise in plywood, multiwood, laminates, veneers, acrylic sheets, adhesives, modular-kitchen accessories and home hardware. Our collection is designed to give architects, contractors, carpenters and homeowners dependable choices for every stage of an interior project.

As authorised dealers for many popular and trusted brands, we focus on quality, reliability and genuine products. Our team helps customers choose materials with the right strength, finish, value and long-term performance for their space.

Useful to know

Frequently asked questions.

01 What are your delivery timelines?+

Delivery depends on product availability and your location in Kerala. After confirmation, our team shares an estimated date and communicates any delay caused by transport disruption, strikes or events outside our control.

02 Can I track my order?+

Online tracking is not currently available. Once an order is out for delivery, we provide the delivery partner’s contact details for direct updates.

03 What is your return process?+

Used products cannot be returned. For reported damage or defects, a brand technician may inspect the product. When the issue is confirmed and approved, PK Holding arranges a replacement or refund.

04 Can you help me choose materials?+

Yes. Tell us about the application, size, finish, budget and location. Our team can help narrow the range and prepare a quotation for suitable products.
